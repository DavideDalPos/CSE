abstract: 'We describe two new species of <i>Keiferia</i> Busck (Lepidoptera: Gelechiidae): <i>K. trixivora</i> Eiseman and Landry, new species, from the USA (California), Mexico, and Costa Rica, whose larvae are leafminers of <i>Trixis californica</i> Kellogg (Asteraceae), and <i>K. feldmani</i> Eiseman and Landry, new species, from the USA (Massachusetts and North Carolina), a leafminer of <i>Solanum carolinense</i> L. (Solanaceae). A tree hypothesis based on maximum-likelihood analysis of the COI barcode region places <i>K. trixivora</i> in monophyly with respect to <i>K. azapaensis</i> Vargas from Chile, whose host plant is <i>Trixis cacalioides</i> D. Don. <i>Keiferia feldmani</i> is in monophyly with respect to <i>K. inconspicuella</i> (Murtfeldt) from the eastern USA, which feeds on <i>Solanum</i> L. species including <i>S. carolinense</i>.'
