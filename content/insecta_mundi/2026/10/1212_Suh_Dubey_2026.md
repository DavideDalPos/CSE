abstract: 'Five whitefly species (Hemiptera: Aleyrodidae), including an undescribed species, were detected at South Korean ports of entry on imported <i>Pieris japonica</i> (Thunb.) D. Don ex G. Don plants originating from China and Japan. These species were identified as <i>Aleuroclava hikosanensis</i> (Takahashi), <i>Aleuroclava similis</i> (Takahashi), <i>Aleuroplatus</i> sp. (cf. <i>joholensis</i>), <i>Cockerelliella psidii</i> (Corbett) and <i>Singhiella pieris</i> Suh and Dubey, new species. This new species is described and illustrated with photographs. In addition, the remaining four species are briefly characterized and illustrative photographs are provided to support accurate identification. This study highlights the importance of monitoring ornamental plant imports to prevent the introduction of high-risk invasive species.'
