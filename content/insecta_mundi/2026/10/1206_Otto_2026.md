abstract: 'Three new fossil eucnemid taxa (Coleoptera: Elateroidea) are described from amber deposits excavated from the vicinity of Santiago, Dominican Republic. The following new species are described: <i>Phlegon femoralis</i>, <i>Adelothyreus micros</i> and <i>Nematodes breviangularis</i>. Each new species is diagnosed and illustrated. <i>Calyptocerus epicranus</i> (Poinar) is transferred to the genus <i>Otho</i> Lacordaire, forming the new combination <i>Otho epicranus</i> (Poinar). The resulting change in the status of the extinct eucnemid creates a change in the status of <i>Lissantauga</i> Poinar becoming a new junior synonym to <i>Otho</i>.'
