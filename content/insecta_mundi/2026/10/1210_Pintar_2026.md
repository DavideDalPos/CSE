abstract: 'Records and notes on the distribution of 91 species of aquatic and semi-aquatic Coleoptera are provided for North America. This includes 56 species of Dytiscidae, 4 species of Epimetopidae, 2 species of Gyrinidae, 3 species of Haliplidae, 1 species of Hydraenidae, 23 species of Hydrophilidae, and 2 species of Noteridae. New country records include <i>Berosus punctatissimus</i> LeConte (Hydrophilidae) in Canada (southwestern British Columbia), <i>Helochares maculicollis</i> Mulsant (Hydrophilidae) in Canada (southern Ontario), <i>Hydrophilus ensifer</i> Brullé (Hydrophilidae) in Belize and Jamaica, <i>Hydrophilus insularis</i> Laporte de Castelnau (Hydrophilidae) in El Salvador and Honduras, and <i>Neoclypeodytes moroni</i> Arce-Pérez and Novelo-Gutiérrez (Dytiscidae) in the United States (southern Texas).'
