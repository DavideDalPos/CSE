abstract: 'New country records are presented for spongillaflies (Neuroptera: Sisyridae) including <i>Sisyra apicalis</i> Banks, 1908 from Ecuador, <i>Sisyra bella</i> Monserrat, 1981 from India, <i>Sisyra indica</i> Needham, 1909 from Pakistan, <i>Sisyra panama</i> Parfin and Gurney, 1956 from Guadeloupe (Lesser Antilles), and <i>Climacia chapini</i> Parfin and Gurney, 1956 from Costa Rica. The record of <i>Sisyra indica</i> from Pakistan represents the first record of the family Sisyridae in the country, while the occurrence of <i>Sisyra panama</i> in Guadeloupe represents the first record of the family Sisyridae from the Lesser Antilles. The known distributions of <i>Sisyra bella</i> Monserrat, 1981 in Sri Lanka and <i>Sisyra indica</i> Needham, 1909 in India are also updated.'
