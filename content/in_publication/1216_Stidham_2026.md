abstract: 'A unique new species of katydid, <i>Arethaea eleanorae</i> (Orthoptera: Tettigoniidae: Phaneropterinae: <i>Arethaea</i> Stål), is described from the Big Bend region of western Texas. The females of this new species are particularly notable for their contrasting white and green abdominal bands.'
