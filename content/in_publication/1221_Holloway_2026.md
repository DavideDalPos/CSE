abstract: 'A new species, <i>Anthrenus</i> (<i>Anthrenus</i>) <i>mahieae</i> (Coleoptera: Dermestidae: Megatominae), from California, U.S.A. is described and compared with <i>Anthrenus lepidus</i> LeConte. Images of habitus (dorsal and ventral), scale structure, antenna, aedeagus, and (male) sternite IX are presented. <i>Anthrenus mahieae</i> differs from <i>A. lepidus</i> in the structure of the genitalia, the structure of the antennal club, and the brightness of the pale patches of scales on the outer pronotal angles and ventrites.'
