abstract: 'We report the first Georgia, USA, records for the relapsing fever tick, <i>Ornithodoros turicata americanus</i> Marx, a gopher tortoise (<i>Gopherus polyphemus</i> Daudin) burrow commensal and proven vector for human relapsing fever spirochetes and African Swine Fever Virus. During May–June 2026, we documented <i>O. t. americanus</i> at 14 sites (in 8 counties) located in the Atlantic Coastal Plain of southeastern Georgia. These collections extend the known range of the tick from northeastern Florida 118 km to the north. We surveyed for <i>O. t. americanus</i> by scooping, then sifting, sand from inside active adult tortoise burrows. At sites where we found <i>O. t. americanus</i>, ticks were present in 68/127 (53.5%) of burrows sampled. We collected 252 <i>O. t. americanus</i> comprised of 27 males, 37 females, 184 nymphs and 4 larvae. Based on negative survey data, the northern range limit for <i>O. t. americanus</i> are sand ridges associated with the Altamaha River. <i>Ornithodoros turicata americanus</i> may also occur in southcentral and southwestern Georgia, and we recommend further gopher tortoise burrow surveys targeting this tick in these regions.'
