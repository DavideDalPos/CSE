abstract: 'The generic classification of North American (Canada, United States of America and Baja California, Mexico) Peritelini is here reassessed. <i>Gironella</i> Anderson, new genus is proposed here to accommodate <i>Geodercodes hispidus</i> Horn, 1894 as <i>Gironella hispida</i> (Horn, 1894), new combination. <i>Aragsilus</i> Anderson, new genus is proposed to accommodate <i>Aragsilus truncatisetosus</i> Anderson, new species, proposed for the species previously widely misidentified as <i>Aragnomus griseus</i> Horn, 1976, and <i>Aragnomus setosus</i> Van Dyke, 1936, now <i>Aragsilus setosus</i> (Van Dyke, 1936), new combination. New synonymies are here proposed in Peritelini as follows: <i>Eucyllus</i> Horn, 1876 is a new senior subjective synonym of <i>Dysticheus</i> Horn, 1876, <i>Rhypodes</i> Horn, 1876 (not Stäl, 1868), <i>Rhypodillus</i> Cockerell, 1906 and <i>Thinoxenus</i> Horn, 1876; <i>Geodercodes</i> Casey, 1888 is a new senior subjective synonym of <i>Nemocestes</i> Van Dyke, 1936; <i>Paraptochus</i> Seidlitz, 1868 is a new senior subjective synonym of <i>Aragnomus</i> Horn, 1876, <i>Peritelodes</i> Casey, 1888, <i>Stenoptochus</i> Casey, 1888, <i>Orthoptochus</i> Casey, 1888 and <i>Anchitelus</i> Casey, 1888. These actions reduce the number of recognized genera of Peritelini in the North American region to nine from fifteen. <i>Peritelus sellatus</i> Boheman, 1859 (now <i>Paraptochus sellatus</i>) is a new senior subjective synonym of <i>Aragnomus griseus</i> Horn, 1876 and <i>Eucilinus aridus</i> (Van Dyke, 1938) is a new senior subjective synonym of <i>Eucyllus tinkhami</i> Tanner, 1959. The following new combinations result from these synonymies: <i>Eucyllus brevicollis</i> (Horn, 1876) (<i>Rhypodes</i>), new combination; <i>Eucyllus dilatatus</i> (Horn, 1876) (<i>Rhypodes</i>), new combination; <i>Eucyllus insignis</i> (Horn, 1876) (<i>Dysticheus</i>), new combination; <i>Eucyllus rotundicollis</i> (Van Dyke, 1953) (<i>Dysticheus</i>), new combination; <i>Eucyllus squalens</i> (Horn, 1876) (<i>Thinoxenus</i>), new combination; <i>Geodercodes expansus</i> (Van Dyke, 1938) (<i>Nemocestes</i>), new combination; <i>Geodercodes fragariae</i> (Van Dyke, 1953) (<i>Nemocestes</i>), new combination; <i>Geodercodes horni</i> (Van Dyke, 1936) (<i>Nemocestes</i>), new combination; <i>Geodercodes incomptus</i> (Van Dyke, 1936) (<i>Nemocestes</i>), new combination; <i>Geodercodes koebelei</i> (Van Dyke, 1936) (<i>Nemocestes</i>), new combination; <i>Geodercodes longulus</i> (Van Dyke, 1936) (<i>Nemocestes</i>), new combination; <i>Geodercodes montanus</i> (Van Dyke, 1936) (<i>Nemocestes</i>), new combination; <i>Geodercodes puncticollis</i> (Casey, 1888) (<i>Geoderces</i>), new combination; <i>Geodercodes tuberculatus</i> (Van Dyke, 1936) (<i>Nemocestes</i>), new combination; <i>Paraptochus hispidulus</i> (Casey, 1888) (<i>Aragnomus</i>), new combination; <i>Paraptochus obtectus</i> (Casey, 1888) (<i>Peritelodes</i>), new combination; <i>Paraptochus alboviridis</i> (Van Dyke, 1936) (<i>Anchitelus</i>), new combination; <i>Paraptochus squamiger</i> (Casey, 1888) (<i>Orthoptochus</i>), new combination; <i>Paraptochus vanduzeei</i> (Van Dyke, 1935) (<i>Stenoptochus</i>), new combination. In addition, <i>Thricolepis simulator</i> Horn, 1876 is here placed as <i>Paraptochus simulator</i> (Horn, 1876), new combination and <i>Eucilinus hirsutus</i> Hatch, 1971 is here placed as <i>Geodercodes hirsutus</i> (Hatch, 1971), new combination. A neotype is designated for <i>Dysticheus insignis</i> Horn, 1876. New lectotypes are designated for <i>Thinoxenus squalens</i> Horn, 1876, <i>Rhypodes dilatatus</i> Horn, 1876, <i>Geodercodes latipennis</i> Casey, 1888, <i>Geoderces incomptus</i> Horn, 1876, <i>Peritelinus variegatus</i> Casey, 1888, <i>Thricolepis inornata</i> Horn, 1876, and <i>Thricolepis seminuda</i> Horn, 1894. A revised key to genera of North American Peritelini is provided.'
