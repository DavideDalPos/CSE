abstract: 'A maximum likelihood phylogenetic tree based on mitochondrial COI data shows that <i>Strephonota syedra</i> (Hewitson, 1867) and <i>Strephonota adela</i> (Staudinger, 1888) (Lycaenidae: Theclinae) form a well-supported clade together with three additional lineages whose taxonomic status requires revision or description. Comparative analyses of morphological characters, mitochondrial DNA, and genomic data derived from type material support the reinstatement of <i>Strephonota jyg</i> Faynel, 2003, reinstated status (type locality: French Guiana), previously considered a synonym of <i>S. syedra</i>, whose type locality is Brazil. The two species are sympatric in French Guiana and Peru. A lectotype is designated for <i>S. syedra</i> and the female of <i>S. jyg</i> is described for the first time. <i>Strephonota chuchuvi</i> Faynel, Busby and Fåhraeus, new species is described based on a male and female from Ecuador (Esmeraldas and Pichincha) and material from Mexico, Guatemala, Costa Rica and Panama. This species is closely related to <i>S. syedra</i> but has several distinguishing wing pattern characters. <i>Strephonota apaza</i> Faynel, Fåhraeus and Siebel, new species is described based on a male holotype from Bolivia and material from Peru, Brazil, Bolivia. This species is sympatric with <i>S. adela</i> in Peru (Loreto), shares a similar wing pattern, but differs in the ventral forewing androconial spot. The female of <i>S. adela</i> is described for the first time, and a lectotype is designated for this species.'
