abstract: 'The scabrous tiger beetle, <i>Cicindelidia scabrosa</i> (Schaupp) (Coleoptera: Carabidae), and the moustached tiger beetle, <i>Ellipsoptera hirtilabris</i> (LeConte) (Coleoptera: Carabidae) are locally distributed, psammophilic species native to the southeastern Coastal Plain of the United States. We report the first Georgia records for <i>C. scabrosa</i>, all of which are near the Atlantic Coast. Field surveys conducted in 2026 indicate that, farther inland, <i>C. scabrosa</i> is replaced geographically by the eastern pine barrens tiger beetle, <i>Cicindelidia abdominalis</i> (Fabricius). We document the first South Carolina record for <i>E. hirtilabris</i> and discuss its distribution in Georgia.'
